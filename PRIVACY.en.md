# QR Snap Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026.

QR Snap accesses selected text, links, or the current tab URL, or captures the visible tab, only when you activate the toolbar or context menu to generate or recognize a QR code. You may also choose to upload a local image. Website content and images may contain personal information; use the extension only on material you are authorized to process.

This data is processed locally in your browser. To pass an action from the toolbar or context menu to the result page, the extension temporarily stores its data in Chrome's `storage.session`. The result page removes it immediately after reading it. The extension has no account system, does not collect usage logs, and does not send images, URLs, selected text, or decoded results to the developer or third parties. It does not use or sell data for advertising.

The extension uses this data only to provide the QR scanning and generation actions you request. It does not use the data for unrelated purposes, personalized advertising, or credit decisions. The developer cannot inspect this locally processed data. These practices follow the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements.

The `activeTab`, `contextMenus`, `scripting`, and `storage` permissions support only these functions. Copying content to the clipboard and opening decoded links require your action.

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project's GitHub repository. Changes to this policy will be reflected in the extension version and store listing.
