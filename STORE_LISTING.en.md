# Chrome Web Store listing copy (English)

This is draft English copy for a future bilingual store update. The v0.1.0 submission uses the [Chinese listing](STORE_LISTING.md).

## Name

QR Snap · Scan and Generate QR Codes

## Short description

Scan QR codes in website images or a selected screen area. Generate QR codes for URLs, links, and selected text. Processed locally.

## Detailed description

Scan a QR code on a website without reaching for your phone.

Right-click an image and choose “QR Snap → Scan QR code in image” to see its contents, copy the text, or choose to open a link. For screenshots, backgrounds, or pages with multiple QR codes, capture the visible tab and drag over the area you want to scan. You can also upload a local image.

To share content, open the toolbar popup to generate a QR code for the current URL or your own text. You can also generate codes from a page, link, or selected text with the right-click menu. Copy the generated image or download it as a PNG.

Images and text are processed locally in the browser. No account or cloud scanning service is needed. The extension accesses the current tab only after you use its toolbar or context menu.

Screen captures cover the visible viewport only; the extension does not scroll the entire page. Decoded links never open automatically.
